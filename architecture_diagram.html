Step 3
The Investigation Engine
Decision Engine
Guides Every Investigation

Decides what needs to happen next for each incident, runs each investigation step in order, and pauses whenever a person needs to make a decision. If an incident recurs after its earlier fix was already marked done, the engine escalates to a person and, once acknowledged, reopens the case and sends it through the investigation team again.

Step 4
The Investigation Team (AI-Assisted)
01Check If This Happened Before

Searches past cases and asks the AI whether one of them plausibly explains this new incident.

02Gather the Evidence

Collects the incident details, system logs, and the related deployment record.

03Build a Timeline

Puts everything in time order and highlights the moment things went wrong.

04Find the Root Cause

Writes an explanation of what most likely caused the issue, backed by evidence.

05Recommend Recovery Steps

Writes clear, step-by-step instructions to fix the issue.

06Draft Resolution & Prevention

Prepares a fair message for the customer and a plan to stop it happening again.

07Publish to Knowledge Base

Saves the finished, approved case so it can help with the next similar incident.

↻ Loop-Back Scenario

When a recurring incident's earlier prevention step was already marked Completed but the problem came back anyway, the Decision Engine escalates to a person for acknowledgement, then reopens the case and re-runs the investigation team from Gather the Evidence onward — this time with the earlier fix on record. If the earlier fix was never finished, no re-investigation happens; the system just notifies the owner to complete it.
