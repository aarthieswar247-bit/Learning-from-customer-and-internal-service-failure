How It Works

The Investigation Process

How every incident is investigated, step by step. There are three possible paths depending on what kind of case it is — and a person always reviews the outcome before anything is finalized.

Starting Point
A Person Opens an Incident and Clicks "Analyze"
Always Happens First
Check If This Happened Before
First checks whether this exact incident was already investigated. If not, searches past cases and asks the AI whether one of them plausibly explains what's happening now.
Decision Point
What Kind of Case Is This?
↙ Similar case found before        ↓ Same issue happening again        ↘ Brand new issue
Case 1
Similar Case Found
01
Gather the Evidence
Collects the incident details, system logs, and the related deployment record.
02
Build a Timeline
Puts everything in time order and highlights the moment things went wrong.
03
Find the Root Cause
Writes an explanation of what most likely caused the issue, backed by evidence.
04
Recommend Recovery Steps
Writes clear, step-by-step instructions to fix the issue, plus a way to confirm it worked.
05
Draft Resolution & Prevention
Prepares a fair message for the customer and a plan to stop it happening again.
06
Expert Review
Pauses here. The root cause and the prevention plan are reviewed and approved separately — both have to be approved before the case can move on.
⏸ Waiting for a Person (2 approvals)
Were both reviews approved?
No → Rejected
Yes ↓
07
Assign Prevention Task
Assigns the prevention plan to an owner with a due date, using what the expert entered during the Prevention approval.
08
Publish to Knowledge Base
Saves the finished, approved case so it can help with future incidents.
Case Closed — Published ✅
Case 2
Happening Again
01
Retrieve the Past Investigation
Pulls up the earlier investigation for this same issue.
02
Check the Earlier Fix
Checks whether the fix from last time was ever actually finished.
03
Notify the Expert
Pauses here and shows the expert why this incident recurred and what the earlier fix was.
⏸ Waiting for a Person
Case Closed — Owner Notified, No Re-Investigation
Case 3
Brand New Issue
01
Request Details from Support person

02

Yes ↓
03
